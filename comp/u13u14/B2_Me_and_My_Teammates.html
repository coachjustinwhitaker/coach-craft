Training Block 2

Me & My Teammates

Create advantage. Move the ball forward. Solve pressure together.
Combination Play Overloads Supporting Attack Primary Intro/Dev Universal — App by Function
Blocks: B1 · Me & the Ball B2 · Teammates B3 · Defending B4 · The Game B5 · The Moment

Combination play only works if the individual technique from B1 is already in place. A player who cannot control the ball under light pressure cannot execute a wall pass at game speed. B2 takes that individual foundation and asks players to solve pressure together: recognizing a teammate's run, providing the right angle of support, and timing the next action so the advantage compounds rather than stalls.

Primary game problem: Players use teammates to create time, space, and numerical advantages, progressing the ball forward under pressure through deliberate combination play.

Core Focus

  • Supporting angle, distance, and timing
  • Combination play to break defensive lines
  • Recognizing and exploiting overloads
  • Movement after the pass, not just the pass itself

Game Moments

  • Attacking — primary
  • Att → Def transition — counter-press in pairs
  • Def → Att transition — secondary

US Soccer U13 Anchor

  • Overload situations centrally and wide
  • CB penetrations into midfield
  • Midfield interchange
  • Quick combination play under high pressure

Application Groups: Who Combines Together

Center Backs + Defensive Mid Playing out of pressure, recycling, CB penetration into midfield
Center Backs + Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Defensive Mid Defensive combinations, covering relationships, playing out wide
Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids + Wingers Overlaps, underlaps, wide combinations connecting defense to attack
Attacking Mids + Wingers + Strikers Breaking lines, overloads, finishing combinations
Skill Areas by Level
Perception & Preparation
Introduction — Universal
  • Scan beyond the ball to identify nearby teammates
  • Recognize simple support positions: behind, beside, and ahead of the ball
  • Identify when a teammate is under pressure and needs an outlet
Development — Universal
  • Scan to identify numbers-up situations before they develop
  • Recognize defensive pressure cues that signal the need to combine rather than carry
  • Anticipate teammate movement rather than reacting to it late
Application — By Function
CBs + Defensive Mid

Recognize when the back line is under press and identify the Defensive Mid as the first release; read when the press is broken and the CB can drive forward.

CBs + Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Defensive Mid

Scan to identify which wide player has pushed forward and which has held, and adjust position to maintain coverage and options behind the ball.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids + Wingers

Recognize when the Winger has drawn the defender inside and the overlap space has opened on the outside.

Attacking Mids + Wingers + Strikers

Identify the third-man run before the combination starts, not after the first pass is made.

Supporting
Introduction — Universal
  • Offer a clear passing option when a teammate has the ball
  • Move to avoid standing directly behind a defender
  • Reposition after passing rather than watching what happens next
Development — Universal
  • Provide support at the correct angle, distance, and timing for the specific pressure the ball carrier faces
  • Move to open space rather than toward the ball when the ball carrier has time
  • Drop short or push in behind based on what the ball carrier needs, not what is most comfortable
Application — By Function
CBs + Defensive Mid

The Defensive Mid drops into the space between the CBs under the press; the CBs split wide to create the triangle and force the press to commit to one side.

CBs + Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Defensive Mid

When the CB has the ball, the Fullback/Wing Back provides the wide outlet and the Defensive Mid provides the central option; the CB reads which channel is open.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids + Wingers

The Attacking Mid supports the wide combination by checking in to create a third option, keeping the combination alive if the first two options are taken away.

Attacking Mids + Wingers + Strikers

The Striker drops short to pull the defender and create space for the Attacking Mid or Winger to run in behind; the supporting movement creates the advantage, not the pass.

Technique — Functional for Combination Play
Introduction — Universal
  • Execute basic wall passes at reduced speed
  • Use a simple supporting movement after passing
  • Adjust passing technique to play to feet rather than space in combination situations
Development — Universal
  • Execute wall passes, overlaps, and simple third-man runs at game speed
  • Use first touch to set up the next combination action, not just to control
  • Adjust passing weight and surface for a moving teammate rather than a stationary one
Application — By Function
CBs + Defensive Mid

Execute the short combination under high press with one or two touches; technique must be clean enough that the press cannot recover between passes.

CBs + Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Defensive Mid

The Fullback/Wing Back receives and plays back into the Defensive Mid with a first-time pass, then continues the run; quick enough that the defender cannot track both ball and run simultaneously.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids + Wingers

Execute the overlap or underlap combination at pace; both players' first touches must set up forward momentum, not slow the combination down.

Attacking Mids + Wingers + Strikers

Execute the third-man combination cleanly under defensive pressure; the lay-off is weighted to the arriving player's run, not to where they are standing.

Passing & Movement
Introduction — Universal
  • Pass and then move to a new supporting position rather than watching the pass
  • Offer a clear option when a teammate has the ball
  • Understand basic spacing around the ball carrier
Development — Universal
  • Pass with purpose and move immediately to unbalance the defensive shape
  • Time the run after passing so it arrives when the ball is ready to come back
  • Recognize when to move toward the ball and when to move away from it to create space
Application — By Function
CBs + Defensive Mid

After playing into the Defensive Mid, the CB moves to continue the build-up as a forward option or covers the space vacated if the Def Mid turns and drives.

CBs + Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Defensive Mid

After the Fullback/Wing Back passes into the Defensive Mid, they continue the run forward; the movement after the pass is the combination, not the pass itself.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids + Wingers

After the overlap run, the Fullback/Wing Back continues toward the endline or cuts inside; the Winger or AM plays the ball in front of the run, not into the feet.

Attacking Mids + Wingers + Strikers

After the Striker lays off, they immediately move to a new position to become the third-man option; the movement after the lay-off creates the next chance.

Decision-Making
Introduction — Universal
  • Recognize the basic choice between combining with a teammate and carrying forward individually
  • Begin choosing between pass-and-move and carry based on where the defender is
  • Make simple combination decisions without waiting to be told
Development — Universal
  • Decide quickly whether to combine, carry, or play forward based on the defensive shape in front
  • Recognize overload situations and choose the combination that exploits them
  • Read when the combination is available and when to abandon it and play safe
Application — By Function
CBs + Defensive Mid

Decide whether to play through the Defensive Mid or carry forward as a CB penetration based on where the press has committed; if the press commits to the Def Mid, the CB drives into the vacated space.

CBs + Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Defensive Mid

The Fullback/Wing Back decides whether to combine short with the CB or play long into the Attacking Mid based on whether the Defensive Mid is marked; the decision is made before receiving, not after.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids + Wingers

Decide whether the overlap or underlap is on based on which defender is positioned higher; the decision is made off the ball, not while carrying it.

Attacking Mids + Wingers + Strikers

Decide whether to play the third-man combination or play forward directly based on how the defenders have positioned relative to the run; if the run is tracked, play forward, if the run is free, play the third-man.
