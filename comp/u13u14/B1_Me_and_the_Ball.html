Training Block 1

Me & the Ball

Prepare the body. Read the picture. Execute with intent.
Perception Technique Playing Forward Attack Primary Universal — All Positions
Blocks: B1 · Me & the Ball B2 · Teammates B3 · Defending B4 · The Game B5 · The Moment

Every player’s relationship with the ball comes before everything else. B1 establishes the perceptual and technical habits that make all combination play, defending, and finishing possible: scanning before the ball arrives, shaping the body to play forward, and using first touch to create a decision rather than just react to pressure.

This block runs through every phase of the season because the habits live here. When a player stalls in B3 or B4, look back at B1 first.

Primary game problem: Players must scan, position, and execute first-touch decisions under high pressure in tight spaces. At U13, the skill itself does not change position to position, the environment around it does. First touch is now a decision made under maximum defensive intensity, not just a technical act.

Core Focus

  • Scanning before, during, and after receiving
  • Body shape and movement to play forward
  • Using first touch to create a decision, not just control
  • Technique executed at full U13 game speed and pressure

Game Moments

  • Attacking — primary
  • Def → Att transition — secondary

US Soccer U13 Anchor

  • Controlling, protecting, driving, dribbling, passing
  • Scanning and adapting body shape
  • All executed under high pressure in tight spaces
Skill Areas by Level
Perception & Preparation
Introduction
  • Scan before receiving to identify pressure and options
  • Begin orienting the body to see the field before the ball arrives
  • Recognize the obvious difference between time and immediate pressure
Development
  • Scan twice before every reception: once for pressure, once for the next pass or run
  • Body shape oriented to see the largest number of options before receiving, not just the nearest one
  • Recognize the difference between time to take a touch and a forced one-touch situation before the ball gets there
Application
  • Scan and prepare while a defender is actively closing, not against a static picture
  • Recognize disguised pressure, a defender showing late or from a blind angle, and adjust body shape in the moment it appears
Controlling
Introduction
  • Treat first touch as a decision, not just control
  • Receive across the body to begin opening up the field
  • Use small, controlled touches to set up the next action
Development
  • First touch redirects the ball into space, not just to the foot
  • Receive across the body to open up the field in one motion
  • Control under direct physical pressure without losing the ball under the body
Application
  • Make the first-touch decision in real time against a defender who is committed and moving at speed, not jogging into a passive challenge
  • Recover from a heavy or imperfect touch without panicking or losing the ball
Protecting
Introduction
  • Shield and protect the ball when no forward option exists
  • Use the body between the ball and the defender
  • Hold possession until a forward option opens
Development
  • Shield with the body positioned between the ball and the defender, not just turned away from it
  • Recognize when holding possession is the right call versus when it is just delaying the inevitable
  • Use the arm and body legally to buy a half-second, not to foul
Application
  • Make the shield-or-release decision in real time against a fast-closing, fully committed defender
  • Protect the ball and recover composure after a heavy touch instead of losing it under pressure
Driving
Introduction
  • Attack the ball under pressure, move toward it rather than waiting
  • Push into open space when it is available
  • Begin to play with the head up while moving with the ball
Development
  • Push the ball into space at speed when it is available, head up and aware of the next decision
  • Know the difference between driving to beat a press and driving recklessly into traffic
  • Accelerate out of the first touch rather than touch-and-stop
Application
  • Execute the driving run against a defender who has scouted the tendency and is sitting on it
  • Read when to abandon the drive and recycle the ball when the picture has closed, without forcing it
Dribbling
Introduction
  • Use a first action, touch or feint, to create space
  • Begin recognizing obvious 1v1 moments
  • Show basic coordination when changing direction with the ball
Development
  • Use a move to beat a defender 1v1 when the picture calls for it, not as a habit
  • Change of pace and direction to create real separation
  • Recognize when dribbling solves the problem and when it creates a worse one
Application
  • Execute the 1v1 move against a defender who has scouted the tendency and is set to stop it
  • Read when to abandon the dribble and recycle possession when the picture has closed, without forcing it
Passing
Introduction
  • Play forward when possible, support when not
  • Use one touch when the pass dictates it
  • Begin passing accurately with both feet under light pressure
Development
  • Weight and accuracy on both feet under pressure, not just the strong foot
  • Disguise the pass with body shape so the defender cannot read it early
  • Play first time when the picture demands it, not just when it is comfortable
Application
  • Execute the disguised or first-time pass against a defender actively trying to read body shape and intercept
  • Find the secure release under pressure even when the first option is taken away
Decision-Making
Introduction
  • Base the decision on body shape and scan before contact
  • Recognize forced one-touch situations versus time to take a touch
  • Decisions are deliberate and often reactive at this level
Development
  • Make the right decision fast, not just the right decision
  • Recognize the picture before the ball arrives so the decision is already half-made
  • Hold composure when the first read is wrong and a second option is needed instantly
Application
  • Make a correct, fast decision when the picture changes after the first scan, the defender arrives sooner than expected, a lane closes late
  • Adjust under pressure without hesitation or panic, trusting the preparation already done
