Coach Craft

Season Plan · Fall 2026

18-Week Block Sequence

The fall season runs three six-week phases: Preseason, In-Season, and Peak Performance. Each week runs two training sessions in parallel. Blocks return at higher levels across the season as the spiral deepens, real gaps between repetitions so each return visit tests retention rather than just continuing where the last session left off. Week 18 is flex: players drive their own priorities heading into the break.

The Three Phases
Phase 1 · Weeks 1-6

Preseason

Objective: Set the baseline

No Application-level work yet, there is no real pressure to apply anything against until games start. Players install and repeat the universal habits and technique that every later block depends on: individual technique, combination play, and collective defending shape.

Success looks like: players are comfortable receiving under light pressure, understand basic team mechanics and defensive shape, and show early decision-making rather than hesitation.

Phase 2 · Weeks 7-12

In-Season

Objective: Apply under real pressure

Games provide the pressure that Application requires. B4 and B5 come online, transitions and finishing, while B1-B3 begin their Application reps against real defenders instead of training constraints.

Success looks like: decisions holding up at game speed, transitions triggering without prompting, and finishing chances beginning to convert more consistently.

Phase 3 · Weeks 13-18

Peak Performance

Objective: Perform at the highest level

This is not mastery, it is the point in the season where players are the fittest and best trained they will be before the break. Every block cycles at Application through repeated performance, not new content. Composure and game management are built through reps, not lectures.

Success looks like: composure in finishing moments, informed choices late in games, communication and ownership without prompting.
