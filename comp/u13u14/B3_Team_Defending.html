Training Block 3

Me & My Team Defending

Compact. Coordinated. Aggressive. Defending is not just for defenders.
Pressure-Cover-Balance High Line Compactness Defending Primary Intro/Dev Universal — App by Function
Blocks: B1 · Me & the Ball B2 · Teammates B3 · Defending B4 · The Game B5 · The Moment

Defending is not just for defenders. Strikers set the tone for the entire pressing structure, they're often the first line and the players who trigger the whole team's coordinated press. B3 is where every player learns that winning the ball back is a collective action: the nearest player pressures, teammates cover behind, and the team stays connected and compact to protect dangerous space.

At U13, the standard is no longer just reacting to losing the ball. Teams are expected to defend proactively, a compact, high defensive block that regains possession as high up the field as possible.

Primary game problem: Players create and maintain a compact, high defensive block, applying coordinated pressure-cover-balance to prevent forward play and regain the ball as high as possible.

Core Focus

  • Coordinated pressure-cover-balance
  • Compact shape, vertically and horizontally
  • High defensive line and line management
  • Immediate reaction after losing the ball

Game Moments

  • Defending — primary
  • Att → Def transition — critical

US Soccer U13 Anchor

  • Create defending shape: compact, high front line, high defensive line
  • Build pressure on the ball as a collective unit
  • Prevent the switch, deny the finish

Application Groups: Who Defends Together

Strikers + Attacking Mids First line of the press, triggering the pressing structure, cutting central lanes
Wingers + Fullbacks/Wing Backs Wide press, showing opponents inside, denying the switch of play
Attacking Mids + Defensive Mid Screening the central lane, covering space vacated by the press
Center Backs + Defensive Mid Last line, line management, covering behind a stepping teammate
Skill Areas by Level
Perception & Preparation
Introduction — Universal
  • React to losing the ball with immediate urgency
  • Identify the ball carrier as the first priority
  • Recognize nearby teammates as defensive support
Development — Universal
  • Scan to recognize pressing triggers before the ball arrives at the target
  • Identify dangerous spaces: central channels, shooting lanes, space in behind
  • Recognize the moment pressure has been broken and shape must adjust
Application — By Function
Strikers + Attacking Mids

Recognize the pressing trigger, a heavy touch, a backward pass, a pass into a closed body shape, and initiate the press instantly.

Wingers + Fullbacks/Wing Backs

Scan to see both the ball and the switch option before showing the opponent inside.

Attacking Mids + Defensive Mid

Recognize when the press has been beaten centrally and the passing lane must be screened immediately.

Center Backs + Defensive Mid

Read the line height relative to the ball and recognize when to step and when to hold.

Covering
Introduction — Universal
  • Recognize the importance of helping a teammate who is pressing
  • Begin to understand basic cover position behind the ball
  • Stay connected to the nearest teammate rather than isolated
Development — Universal
  • Provide cover at the correct distance and angle for the specific press in front
  • Recognize when to slide across to cover a teammate who has stepped
  • Communicate cover position so the pressing teammate can commit fully
Application — By Function
Strikers + Attacking Mids

The second Striker or AM covers the passing lane the first press has shown the opponent away from, closing the release before it happens.

Wingers + Fullbacks/Wing Backs

The Fullback/Wing Back covers in behind the Winger's press, ready for the ball played in behind or square.

Attacking Mids + Defensive Mid

The Defensive Mid covers centrally while the AM presses, staying connected enough to prevent the central lane opening.

Center Backs + Defensive Mid

The covering CB reads the stepping CB's press and adjusts the line so no gap opens in behind.

Shape & Line Management
Introduction — Universal
  • Understand that the team defends as a connected group, not individuals
  • Begin to recognize when the team is too spread out
  • Recover into a basic defensive shape after losing the ball
Development — Universal
  • Maintain compactness vertically and horizontally as a unit
  • Shift as a group toward the ball rather than defending in isolation
  • Recognize when the team shape has been broken and needs to reset
Application — By Function
Strikers + Attacking Mids

Set the height of the press and manage the distance to the midfield line so the block doesn't get split.

Wingers + Fullbacks/Wing Backs

Maintain width discipline so the team stays compact centrally without conceding the flank entirely.

Attacking Mids + Defensive Mid

Manage the distance between the midfield and defensive lines, the space most likely to be exploited by combination play.

Center Backs + Defensive Mid

Control the height of the back line, manage the offside trigger, and stay connected to the Defensive Mid above.

Defensive Decision-Making
Introduction — Universal
  • Understand the basic choice between pressing and delaying
  • Begin to recover goal-side when beaten
  • Recognize the importance of helping teammates defend rather than defending alone
Development — Universal
  • Decide when to step, delay, or recover based on the picture in front
  • Shift as a unit toward the ball rather than making an individual decision
  • Deny penetration and force play into less dangerous areas
Application — By Function
Strikers + Attacking Mids

Decide whether to press the ball or screen the pass based on whether the press can arrive before the ball is released.

Wingers + Fullbacks/Wing Backs

Decide whether to show inside or outside based on team shape and where cover is positioned.

Attacking Mids + Defensive Mid

Decide whether to jump the passing lane or hold position based on the risk of being played through.

Center Backs + Defensive Mid

Decide whether to step and engage or hold the line, understanding that stepping wrong breaks the entire defensive structure.
