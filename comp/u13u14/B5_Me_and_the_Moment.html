Training Block 5

Me & the Moment

Can we turn advantage into goals?
Finishing Final Actions Composure Attack Primary Intro/Dev Universal — App by Finishing Role
Blocks: B1 · Me & the Ball B2 · Teammates B3 · Defending B4 · The Game B5 · The Moment

All of the work in B1 through B4 is preparation for this block. Players who scan early, combine well, defend collectively, and transition fast create finishing opportunities. B5 is about having the composure and technique to take them.

When players struggle to finish, the answer is rarely more finishing drills. Check the quality of preparation touches, the timing of runs, and the decisions made before the shot. The problem usually lives earlier in the chain.

Primary game problem: Players convert advantage into goals under high defensive pressure, separating to receive, combining for the final pass, and finishing with composure and limited touches, while managing tempo and stakes as the game state demands.

Core Focus

  • Converting advantage into goals through quality final actions
  • Separating and unmarking to receive in the box
  • Finishing with composure and limited touches under pressure
  • Managing tempo and stakes as the game state demands

Game Moments

  • Attacking — primary
  • Def → Att transition — secondary

US Soccer U13 Anchor

  • Separate and unmark to receive
  • Final pass: through ball or combination play
  • Numbers in the box, take on 1v1, finish with limited touches

Application Groups: Role in the Finishing Sequence

Strikers + Wingers: The Finish The shot or first-time finish under closing pressure
Attacking Mids + Wingers: The Final Pass The through ball or combination that creates the chance
Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids: Delivery from Wide Crosses and cutbacks delivered from deep areas
All Positions: Numbers in the Box / Game State Arriving to finish, composure, and managing the moment
Skill Areas by Level
Recognizing the Moment
Introduction — Universal
  • Identify when in shooting or final-pass range
  • Notice goal location and basic pressure
  • Recognize simple scoring opportunities
Development — Universal
  • Read defender distance and goalkeeper position before acting
  • Distinguish between shooting, passing, or recycling
  • Anticipate second-ball and rebound opportunities
Application — By Finishing Role
Strikers + Wingers: The Finish

Recognize the exact moment a shooting window opens under closing pressure, often less than a second.

Attacking Mids + Wingers: The Final Pass

Recognize when a teammate has separated and the passing lane is live before the defender recovers.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids: Delivery from Wide

Recognize the right moment to deliver, early before the box is set, or late for a cutback once runners arrive.

All Positions: Numbers in the Box / Game State

Recognize when the moment calls for numbers forward and when it calls for restraint based on the score and time remaining.

Preparation & Body Shape
Introduction — Universal
  • Set body to face goal when receiving near the box
  • Use a settling touch to prepare for a shot or pass
  • Show basic balance before striking the ball
Development — Universal
  • Adjust body shape to shoot with one or two touches
  • Prepare for first-time finishes when appropriate
  • Set feet and hips quickly as pressure arrives
Application — By Finishing Role
Strikers + Wingers: The Finish

Prepare the body to strike while still moving at speed, with a defender or goalkeeper closing the angle.

Attacking Mids + Wingers: The Final Pass

Prepare the body to disguise the final pass so the defender cannot read through ball versus cutback.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids: Delivery from Wide

Prepare body shape to strike a moving ball at pace without breaking stride, keeping the delivery options open until the last moment.

All Positions: Numbers in the Box / Game State

Prepare mentally for the moment before it arrives, know your job in the box before the ball is delivered.

Executing Final Actions
Introduction — Universal
  • Use inside foot or simple laces technique to finish
  • Complete uncontested headers or rebounds
  • Execute basic cutbacks or square passes
Development — Universal
  • Finish under light-to-moderate defensive pressure
  • Execute volleys and half-volleys in live play
  • Choose placement vs. power based on the situation
Application — By Finishing Role
Strikers + Wingers: The Finish

Finish through contact and tight windows with limited touches, taking on the defender 1v1 to create or score when the picture calls for it.

Attacking Mids + Wingers: The Final Pass

Execute the through ball or combination pass that breaks the last line under high pressure.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids: Delivery from Wide

Deliver a cross or cutback with the weight and pace to reach a moving target, not just the space.

All Positions: Numbers in the Box / Game State

Arrive in the correct zone in the box to convert a delivery, or manage the ball late to protect the result.

Composure & Ownership
Introduction — Universal
  • Willing to shoot or play the final pass
  • Accept missed chances without frustration
  • Reset quickly after attempts
Development — Universal
  • Stay composed as pressure and stakes increase
  • Take responsibility for decisive moments
  • Respond positively after success or failure
Application — By Finishing Role
Strikers + Wingers: The Finish

Build the habit of refocusing immediately after a missed chance, through repetition.

Attacking Mids + Wingers: The Final Pass

Take ownership of the risk in playing the final ball, reinforced by how it is coached: praise the attempted through ball that gets intercepted as much as the one that connects, so the decision to try stays the same regardless of outcome.

Fullbacks/Wing Backs + Attacking Mids: Delivery from Wide

Build the delivery decision through repetition under time pressure until picking the right ball, cross or cutback, becomes the default rather than a choice made under stress.

All Positions: Numbers in the Box / Game State

Give players a specific job and communication trigger for the moment, who calls the game state, what gets said when protecting a lead versus chasing a goal, so the response is a rehearsed habit, not a personality trait some players have and others don't.
