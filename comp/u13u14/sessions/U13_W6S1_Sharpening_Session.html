Shielding
2v1 with Coach  ·  Introduce and Rep  ·  6–8 minutes
Format
Partners
Circle
~2 ft diameter
Reps
5 rounds, switch roles, 2 sets
Duration
6–8 min
Vocab
Shield
ObjectiveIntroduce shielding before Jukebox so players have the tool before they need it under competitive pressure. Partner passes to partner then immediately becomes the presser. Receiving player collects and places ball in the circle, then shields for as long as possible. Longest shield wins the round. 5 rounds then switch roles. Two sets, 10 shielding reps each player total.
Structure
DemoShow body position first: between the ball and the defender, low center of gravity, knees bent, legal arm use. One slow rep then go live. Keep the demo short, players learn shielding by feeling pressure not by listening.
Set 1Player A passes to Player B then immediately presses. Player B receives, places ball in circle, shields. Hold as long as possible. Longest shield wins the round. 5 rounds, then switch roles. Player B becomes the passer and presser.
Set 2Same format. Second set of 5 rounds each role. By the end every player has 10 shielding reps. Coach calls out good body position as it happens.

Technical  ·  Activity 4 of 5
Finishing Circuit
Three-Shot Sequence  ·  Two Lines  ·  Miss All Three and Run  ·  12–15 minutes
Lines
2 simultaneous
Shots/Rep
3 in sequence
Consequence
Miss all three: run to halfway
Passer
Next player in line
Duration
12–15 min
ObjectiveThree finishing reps per turn in a continuous sequence. Player moves between shots rather than standing and waiting. The next player in line plays the pass for Shot 3 then immediately starts their own rep. Everyone is active, nobody is just watching.
One Rep  ·  Three Shots in Sequence
Shot 1 · Drive and FinishPlayer starts on the left side of the box, drives at pace toward goal and shoots. Power or placement their choice. Get the ball on target.
Shot 2 · Through BallPlayer comes back through the middle, runs around a post, receives a through ball played into the box by the coach. Finish first or second touch.
Shot 3 · Cutback from TeammatePlayer cuts around the far post and receives a pass from the next player in line stationed wide or at the top of the box. Finish from the cutback. That teammate then starts their own rep.

Transition Game  ·  Activity 5 of 5
Transition Game
Half Field  ·  Full Size Goals  ·  1v1 → 2v1 → 3v2 → Reset  ·  20 minutes
Field
Half field
Goals
Full size each end
Keepers
Stay in goal
Offside
Egregious only
Duration
20 min
ObjectiveTrain transition recognition: the moment possession changes, go. Each wave builds numerically so players get reps at 1v1, 2v1, and 3v2 decision-making in quick succession. Walk through one full cycle slowly before going live.
Wave Structure  ·  Repeating Cycle
Wave 1 · 1v1Team A sends one player forward with the ball. Team B sends one defender. 1v1 to the Team B goal. After the shot or turnover, immediately Wave 2.
Wave 2 · 2v1Team B sends one player with the ball plus one additional player. Team A has one defender remaining. 2v1 to the Team A goal. After the shot or turnover, immediately Wave 3.
Wave 3 · 3v2Team A sends two more players, one with the ball. 3v2 back to the Team B goal. After the shot or turnover, quick rest and reset.
ResetQuick rest. Team B now starts Wave 1. Cycle repeats with teams reversed.
Coaching Points
  • Transition recognition: the moment possession changes, go. No pause.
  • 1v1: take the defender on, no help is coming
  • 2v1: get into a useful position early, make the decision easy for the ball carrier
  • 3v2: be patient, the numbers are in your favor, don't force it
  • Defending: delay, stay on your feet, force wide
Vocabulary This Session
Shield
Body between the ball and the defender. Low center of gravity, knees bent, legal arm use. Actively protecting possession rather than just turning your back.
When: receiving with pressure from behind, holding possession when no forward option is available
