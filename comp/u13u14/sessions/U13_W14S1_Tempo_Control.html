Philosophy Connection

Tempo Control is the Week 14 theme. After a game where the team had plenty of the ball but defaulted to direct play against a compact block, the question is how the speed of play gets decided: by the player who sees the space, not by a rule. Today we build the habit of playing and moving, finding the split, and shooting only when the window is open. The back line also gets a rep against long balls and a keeper who punts.

Session Objectives
  • Play and move to a new space after every pass
  • Find the split between defenders and time the run so the runner meets the ball
  • Shoot when the window is open, not just when the ball arrives
  • Back line: read the punt, control it, find the outlet, and play forward within 3 seconds
  • Keep the tempo when the rules come off

Activation · Activity 2 of 5
Rondos with a Movement Rule
Two 5v2 grids, about 15x15 yd · 8 minutes
Setup
Two 5v2 grids
Grid
About 15x15 yd
Duration
8 min
ObjectiveWarm up the passing and plant the tempo idea early: play, then move to a new space. Nobody holds a spot.
Parts
Part 1 · Keep itKeep the ball, play and move. A defender who wins it swaps in for the player who lost it.
Part 2 · New spaceSame rondo with one rule: you cannot hold your spot. After you pass, move to a new open space before the next pass is played.
Coaching Points
  • Where is the open angle before you pass? Pass to where you would want your teammate to be.
  • Are you standing behind a defender or in front of him?
  • If you can do it in one touch, why take two?
  • After you pass, where is the next space you can move to?
3
Small-Sided Game · Activity 3 of 5
3v3+1 to Endzones
Split balls and timed runs · 12 minutes
Setup
2 grids, 3v3+1
Grid
25x25 yd + 5 yd endzones
The +1
Plays for the team with the ball
Duration
12 min
ObjectiveFind the split between the defenders and time the run so the runner meets the ball in the endzone. Speed of play stays a conversation, not a rule.
Rules
EndzoneScore by playing the ball into the endzone for a teammate to meet it. No player can enter the endzone before the ball.
FreezeCoach freezes the game when a player stands still or sits behind a defender. Where was the space?
SpeedNo touch limit. Talk about speed of play instead of forcing it.
Coach Note

The boys read Soccer IQ Chapter 1 (one touch if you can, two if you can't), Chapter 4 (passing angles and empathy) and Chapter 16 (the splits). Use their language. Ask: was there a one-touch option you missed? Where would you want the ball if you were your teammate?

Coaching Points
  • Where was the space before the pass?
  • Was there a one-touch option you missed?
  • Runner: meet the ball in the endzone, do not go early
  • Do not stand behind a defender. Find the space.
4
Split Groups · Activity 4 of 5
Punt Defense and Red Light / Green Light
Two groups run in parallel · 25 minutes · Keepers swap at about 12 minutes
Group A · Back line (CBs, FBs) · Punt Defense
Reps
Rep 1No runners. Server punts, the D reads it and controls. Mids show outlet angles and the D picks.
Rep 2Passive runners. Roles called before the punt: first ball and second ball.
Rep 3Live. Win it, first pass forward to a mid or into space within 3 seconds.
Coaching Points: Back Line
  • Drop a few yards and squeeze up after the first touch, not before
  • Find the outlet before you win it
  • Mids: show the angle, be ready to play forward
Coach Note

Needs a real punt: a keeper or a coach who can hit one. Strikers and wingers are the runners, 2-3 at a time. Mids sit in outlet spots. Group coach is Dennis or Paul, depending on who shows up.

Group B · Mids, wingers, strikers · Red Light / Green Light Finishing
Rules
RedKeep the ball and stay calm. A coach counts out loud.
GreenThe sixth pass opens a 5-second window. The player with the ball calls "green" and shoots from inside the shot zone, 2 touches max. If the window closes with no shot, the count goes back to zero and they need 6 new passes.
ScoreOn target 1, goal 3, over the bar 0. A miss or a turnover sends the 3 defenders and the keeper on a counter to a target at the half line. The attackers have 3 seconds to win it back or drop.
Coach Note

If 5 seconds is too tight, loosen it to 7 and tighten later. It should be rushed enough to force a decision, not so rushed that everyone blasts it. Mark corner targets with cones or small goals. Numbers and grid size are set on the day.

Coaching Points: Top Group
  • What made it a green light? What did you see before the pass?
  • Where was the space before the pass?
  • Who calmed the ball on red, and who sped it up on green?
  • Plant foot beside the ball, head over it, side-foot to a corner

Scrimmage · Activity 5 of 5
Final Game, 7v7 Half Field
Three 10-minute phases · 30 minutes
Teams
7v7 + coach neutrals
Field
Half field, 2 full-size goals
Keepers
Both, swap goals at phase breaks
Duration
30 min
ObjectiveCarry the tempo read from the rondos, the endzone game and the window into a real game, then see what sticks when the rules come off.
Phases
Phase 1 · 10'Red light / green light is live: 6 passes opens a 5-second window, and a shot only counts inside an open window. No punts, so every keeper restart is short.
Phase 2 · 10'Same rules, and splits count double. The runner has to meet the ball, not go early.
Phase 3 · 10'Free scrimmage. One drop ball restart so the 4, 5 and 6 set the shape first.
Coach Note

Coaches play midfield neutrals, one or two touches, and move to open space. If they hold the ball, they become the tempo. Neutrals count toward the 6 passes. Space is tight with 7v7 on half a field and full-size goals, so adjust numbers to what you have.

Coaching Points
  • Freeze it when someone stands behind a defender and ask where the angle was
  • Can you do it in one touch? Then why take two?
  • Phase 3: are they still playing one and two touch without a rule?
