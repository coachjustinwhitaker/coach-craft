Me & the Game

“Recognizing moments and acting fast.”
Transition SpeedGame IntelligenceBoth TransitionsDecision Under Chaos
Blocks: B1 · Me & the BallB2 · TeammatesB3 · DefendingB4 · The GameB5 · The Moment

The moments between defending and attacking are where games are decided. B4 is about reacting before the other team can reorganize: playing forward immediately after regaining possession, recovering shape immediately after losing it.

B4 only works if B1, B2, and B3 are in place. Players who cannot receive cleanly (B1) or combine quickly (B2) will not be able to attack at transition speed. The spiral matters here.

Primary game problem: Players recognize transition moments earlier and respond with speed, intent, and coordination to create advantage or reduce danger.

Core Focus

  • Reacting immediately after winning or losing the ball
  • Making fast decisions in transition moments
  • Coordinating movement to attack with speed or recover shape

Game Moments

  • Att → Def transition — primary
  • Def → Att transition — primary
  • This block explicitly links attack and defense

Key Player Actions

  • Reaction speed
  • Acceleration and deceleration
  • Forward passing on regain
  • Supporting runs
  • Decision-making under chaos
Skill Areas by Level
Perception & Awareness
Introduction
  • Recognize when possession has changed
  • Identify immediate attacking or defensive cues
  • Begin scanning during chaotic moments
Development
  • Understand the idea of playing fast vs. recovering
  • Begin choosing action instead of freezing
  • Learn that hesitation creates disadvantage
Application
  • Read transition cues in real time
  • Recognize game state: risk vs. control
  • Communicate and organize teammates during transitions
Technical & Physical Actions
Introduction
  • React quickly after losing or winning the ball
  • Accelerate toward the ball or space
  • Attempt forward passes after regaining possession
Development
  • Identify numbers-up or numbers-down situations
  • Recognize when space is available to attack
  • Anticipate transition moments before they fully occur
Application
  • Execute forward actions at speed under pressure
  • Transition seamlessly between attacking and defending roles
  • Maintain intensity through repeated transition moments
Decision-Making
Introduction
  • Play forward on regain when space is open, otherwise keep the ball
  • Decide to press or recover based on whether the ball can be won
  • Make the first action quickly instead of waiting
Development
  • Choose between fast attack and possession
  • Recognize when the press has failed and recover
  • Support teammates rather than acting alone
Application
  • Choose speed or control at full speed based on the score and the picture
  • Decide between delaying the counterattack and pressing for the regain
  • Keep composure to find the player in a scoring position at speed

Overall Picture by Level

Introduction

Players begin to recognize that transitions matter. After losing or winning the ball, they react faster, even if decisions are still inconsistent.

Development

Players respond to transitions with intention. Attacks begin quickly after regaining possession and defensive recovery becomes faster and more organized.

Application

Transitions become a strength. The team consistently creates advantage by acting faster and smarter than the opponent during chaotic moments.
