Me & the Ball

“See first. Prepare the body. Create space. Execute with intent.”
Perception Technique Playing Forward Attack Primary
Blocks: B1 · Me & the Ball B2 · Teammates B3 · Defending B4 · The Game B5 · The Moment

Every player’s relationship with the ball comes before everything else. B1 establishes the perceptual and technical habits that make all combination play, defending, and finishing possible: scanning before the ball arrives, shaping the body to play forward, and using first touch or feint to create space rather than react to pressure.

This block runs through every phase of the season because the habits live here. When a player stalls in B3 or B4, look back at B1 first.

Primary game problem: Players develop the ability to scan, position themselves, and use body shape, first action, and movement to create space and play forward under pressure.

Core Focus

  • Scanning before, during, and after receiving
  • Body shape and movement to play forward
  • Using first action (touch or feint) to create space
  • Decision-making on first and second touch

Game Moments

  • Attack — primary
  • Def → Att transition — secondary

Key Player Actions

  • Scanning
  • Adapting body shape and position
  • Controlling and protecting the ball
  • Using body movement to unbalance defenders
  • Passing or dribbling forward
  • Shooting when the chance is there
  • Supporting angles
Skill Areas by Level
Perception & Preparation
Introduction
  • Scan occasionally before receiving to identify pressure or teammates
  • Begin adjusting body shape to face the field when possible
  • Recognize obvious moments of time vs. immediate pressure
Development
  • Scan regularly before receiving under light to moderate pressure
  • Receive on the half-turn when space allows
  • Recognize when one-touch play is needed vs. when they can take a touch
Application
  • Scan early and often, even under active pressure
  • Adjust position before receiving to create time and space
  • Consistently recognize tempo: one-touch, two-touch, or carry
Footwork & Ball Mastery
Introduction
  • Use small, controlled touches to settle the ball
  • Show basic coordination when changing direction
  • Begin using body position to protect the ball when pressured
Development
  • Use first touch to move the ball away from pressure
  • Adjust footwork to receive with inside or outside foot as needed
  • Protect the ball using body shape when pressure arrives
Application
  • First touch consistently creates a new angle or breaks pressure
  • Maintain balance and coordination while changing direction at speed
  • Shield the ball effectively under pressure
Feints & Body Movement
Introduction
  • Use simple body movements to suggest one action and take another
  • Show balance and coordination when shifting weight or changing direction
  • Begin recognizing when a defender is close enough to manipulate
Development
  • Use body feints to commit or delay a defender before first touch
  • Coordinate feint and first touch to move into space
  • Maintain balance and control while changing direction under light pressure
Application
  • Choose between feint, first touch, or immediate pass based on defender position
  • Use body movement to create space to play forward or escape pressure
  • Execute feints at game speed while scanning for next action
Passing & Receiving
Introduction
  • Complete short passes with correct surface
  • Receive the ball with intent to control first
  • Attempt to pass to open teammates rather than defaulting forward
Development
  • Weight and pace the pass so the teammate can continue without slowing down
  • Avoid passing to a teammate surrounded by two or more opponents
  • Receive on the bounce or from the air with a soft first touch
Application
  • Play the right pass at the right speed and time under active pressure
  • Play a long pass in the air to a teammate in space
  • Play first time when the picture demands it and the pass can be accurate
Shooting
Introduction
  • Shoot on goal when the chance is there instead of passing it up
  • Strike the ball cleanly with the laces or inside of the foot
  • Look at the goal and the goalkeeper before shooting
Development
  • Read the goalkeeper's position and select a target before the shot
  • Aim for the far side when shooting from an angle and the goalkeeper protects the near post
  • Shoot after a dribble or a pass, not only from a set ball
Application
  • Finish in one touch when closely marked
  • Keep clean technique and target selection with a defender closing
  • Pick the shot over the extra touch when the window is short
Decision-Making
Introduction
  • Recognize when a forward option exists
  • Begin to understand that keeping possession is sometimes the best choice
  • Decisions are slow but intentional
Development
  • Choose between playing forward or keeping possession more consistently
  • Make decisions faster as comfort increases
  • Begin recognizing simple combination opportunities
Application
  • Play forward or line-breaking passes when available
  • Use appropriate passing surface and weight instinctively
  • Receive the ball to immediately connect play

Overall Picture by Level

Introduction

Players are trying to prepare before the ball arrives.

Development

Players are making repeatable, intentional choices.

Application

Players look comfortable, composed, and connected. Decisions are fast and reliable.
