Every session is built from five blocks

Plan the season by block, so players build skills in an order that makes sense.

  • B1Me & the BallIndividual skill, pressure, scanning, body shape
  • B2Me & My TeammatesPassing, combination play, creating and using space
  • B3Me & My Team DefendingPressing, delaying, covering, marking, staying compact
  • B4Me & the GameBoth transitions: the moment we lose it and the moment we win it
  • B5Me & the MomentFinishing, composure, game management
